Adil Hoque

Motion Graphics Designer • Video Editor

I’m a passionate video editor and motion graphics designer who enjoys transforming ideas into visually engaging stories. I specialize in creating dynamic video edits, smooth transitions, and eye-catching motion graphics that capture attention and keep audiences engaged. I believe every video has a story to tell, and my goal is to bring that story to life through creativity, precision, and attention to detail. I’m constantly exploring new techniques, experimenting with ideas, and refining my skills to deliver work that is both visually appealing and meaningful.

My Journey

01. The Early Days

Coming from a commerce and marketing background, my creative journey began quite differently. As a teenager, I enjoyed making small scripted videos with friends and cousins. I’d write the stories, direct the scenes, and edit the footage myself. What started as a fun activity gradually sparked my interest in visual storytelling and video editing.

02. Discovering Video Editing

What began as a hobby eventually turned into something I wanted to pursue seriously. Around 2024, I started learning video editing professionally, initially with the goal of earning an income. I found myself particularly drawn to commercial videos and promotional reels, where creativity meets visual communication.

03. Making It a Career

As my skills developed, so did my confidence in my work. I realized that video editing was more than just a skill I could use to earn money; it was something I genuinely enjoyed doing. Around 2025, I decided to pursue it as a full-time career and take my creative ambitions more seriously.

04. Beyond Editing

My curiosity eventually led me towards motion graphics and animation, opening up an entirely new dimension of creativity. I’m now exploring the possibilities of combining editing, design, and animation to create more engaging visual experiences. I’m also looking forward to diving deeper into long-form, story-driven content.
